Record your NESP32's log while you play. If a game crashes or two consoles stop with GAMES DIFFERED, the log shows what happened, and this page puts it in a problem report for you.

Plug the NESP32 into this computer with a USB data cable, leave it in the controller port, and press Start.

Report the problem

This browser cannot reach a USB serial port. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer.

This page has to be opened over HTTPS, or from localhost, to reach a USB serial port.

Weren't recording when it crashed? The NESP32 keeps its last crash dump, and tells it again a second after the console is switched on or reset. Press the console's Reset button, or restart the NESP32. Either ends a game being played.
