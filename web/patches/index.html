How to patch

RomPatcher.js, in your browser

  1. Open RomPatcher.js.
  2. Choose your game's .nes file as the ROM file.
  3. Choose the game's .bps from this page as the patch.
  4. Press Apply patch. It saves the patched ROM.

Floating IPS

  1. Press Apply Patch and choose the .bps.
  2. Choose your game's .nes file.
  3. Choose where to save the patched ROM.

Copy the patched ROM to your flash cartridge's SD card or your MiSTer as you would any NES game.

If the patcher says the ROM is wrong (a checksum or CRC mismatch), your copy is a different release from the one listed above, or has no iNES header. The patch isn't broken: find the right release.

In Case of Trouble

  • Both players need the same patched version or you will get a "NOT THIS GAME" error in the lobby.
