nesp32

Wi-Fi, files and a second console for a Nintendo Entertainment System, on an M5Stack StickS3 in controller port 2. This page puts the firmware on a StickS3 from the browser.

Connect the StickS3 to this computer with a USB cable that carries data, and press the button.

This browser cannot reach a USB serial port. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. This page has to be served over HTTPS, or from localhost, to reach a USB serial port.

Before you do

  • It replaces what is on the StickS3, UIFlow included. M5Burner brings UIFlow back.
  • The browser asks whether to erase first. Answer no when updating a nesp32, and it keeps its networks and its music.
  • If the StickS3 is not in the browser's list, it may be showing as a USB drive: leave Setup's USB screen, or switch it off and on.

Afterwards

  1. Fit the hat and plug the nesp32 into controller port 2.
  2. Start the cartridge and join a network from Setup, with the controller. Nothing else is set up from a computer.

More

The hat, the cartridge and the source are at github.com/Armitage64/nesp32.
